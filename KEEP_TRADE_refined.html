Demo game · Target RTP 97% · No real prizes

Two choices. One reveal.

  1. Enter your bet and tap PLAY.
  2. Your mystery box is on the left. The visible offer is on the right. KEEP BOX wins if your box is worth more. TAKE OFFER wins if the visible offer is worth more.
  3. After a win, CASH OUT banks the displayed amount. CONTINUE risks all of it on a new comparison.

There is no stage limit. Continue after any win or start another round after a loss or cash-out. A loss ends the streak and forfeits the round's payout. Bets are locked during a round.

Odds & payouts

Start a round to see the current choice probabilities.

Each choice button shows the total payout multiplier and the resulting dollar payout, including your original bet. For example, a $10 bet at 2.80× pays $28 in total, not $28 plus the bet. Win probabilities for the current reveal are shown below; opening Settings does not change the outcome. Box contents are randomly drawn and fixed before your decision. Values never tie.

The first multiplier is 0.97 ÷ your selected win probability. After that, the accumulated multiplier is divided by each new selected probability. For any fixed finite stage count, theoretical return is 97% before rounding; continuing indefinitely does not guarantee a payout. The demo has no certified RTP audit.

Payouts include the original stake. Cash-out settles to the nearest cent; multipliers shown are rounded for display. Brand names and fictional dollar values are decorative. No real money or goods can be won. Streak badges are cosmetic: they add no bonus payout and do not improve your next win probability.
