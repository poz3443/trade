How to play

👀

You always see the next egg first. Before every risk, the Cluckasaurus holds up the egg on offer — its class and how far it would push your multiplier if the bandit catches it.

📈

Every egg multiplies your win — there's no ceiling. Each catch scales your multiplier up, so a hot streak can climb into the hundreds or thousands of ×. Keep going as far as you dare.

⏱️

You then get a short window. Do nothing and the bandit goes for the egg. Or hit CASH OUT to bank bet × multiplier and walk before you risk a thing. The ring shows the time left.

🥚

Three egg classes, three temptations:
Normal — small, safe steps.
Blue — a bigger jump, a bit more risk.
Golden — a huge leap… and the lowest catch chance. The classic "do I risk all of this?" moment.

💥

Bigger egg, bigger reward — but a lower chance of catching it. If it cracks, the whole basket spills and the round is lost.

🛡

Provably-fair ready. Each round's result is fixed by a seed before any egg flies, so the reveal never lies and nothing changes mid-round. (This demo uses a local seed; a live build would combine server + client seeds.)

Demo credits only — no real money. 98% theoretical RTP, verified over a million rounds. The catch you see is just the pre-decided result being played out. Press D for the debug panel and RTP simulator.
